I am a PhD researcher in the **Symmetric Cryptography Group at Ruhr University Bochum**, supervised by **Prof. Gregor Leander**. My research covers the analysis and design of symmetric cryptographic primitives, including block ciphers, hash functions, and pseudorandom generators.

I combine mathematical analysis with experiments and automated tools in **CUDA, C/C++, Python, and SageMath**, including SAT- and MILP-based methods. My publications include works at **CRYPTO**, **EUROCRYPT**, and **ASIACRYPT**. I plan to submit my PhD thesis in November 2026, with my defense expected in March 2027.

## Research interests

- **Symmetric cryptography:** block ciphers, hash functions, pseudorandom generators, cryptanalysis, key-recovery attacks, and cipher design.
- **Hardware implementation:** GPU optimization and side-channel masking, including threshold implementations.
- **AI security:** cryptanalytic attacks on AI and its components, and neural-network model extraction.

See my [publications]({{ '/publications/' | relative_url }}) and [talks]({{ '/talks/' | relative_url }}) for recent work.
